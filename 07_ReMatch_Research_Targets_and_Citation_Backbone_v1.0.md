## 5. Citation hygiene rules

- Use SAMHSA/NIDA/HUD/CDC/FDA/NIJ/NIC sources for official claims.
- Use peer-reviewed sources for mechanisms and intervention evidence.
- Avoid overclaiming neuroplasticity.
- Use RNR with caveats.
- Distinguish navigation from treatment.
- Cite all prevalence and current landscape data with dates.
- Separate theoretical support from tested Re.Match outcomes until pilot data exist.
