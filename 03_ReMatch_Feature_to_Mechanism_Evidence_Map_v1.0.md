## Interpretation

The evidence map shows that Re.Match is not a single-theory product. It is a multi-mechanism system. Its strength is the way multiple well-documented mechanisms converge:

1. Recovery is multidimensional, not merely abstinence.
2. Mental-health recovery requires identity, connection, meaning, hope, and empowerment.
3. Trauma-informed systems must preserve safety, voice, and choice.
4. Addiction recovery is shaped by treatment access, stress, reward, routine, executive function, and environment.
5. Reentry requires dynamic life-domain development, not only surveillance.
6. Housing, documents, benefits, food, transport, and employment are behavioral-health determinants.
7. Implementation succeeds or fails based on context, fit, adoption, feasibility, fidelity, and sustainment.
